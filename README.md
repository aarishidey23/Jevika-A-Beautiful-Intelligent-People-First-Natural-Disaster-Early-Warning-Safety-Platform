🌍 JEEVIKA – Disaster Preparedness & Humanitarian Safety Platform

Be Prepared. Stay Informed. Stay Safe.

📌 About the Project

JEEVIKA is a disaster preparedness and humanitarian safety platform designed to help individuals and communities stay informed, prepared, and connected during emergencies.

Natural disasters and unexpected emergencies can put lives at risk, especially when people lack access to timely information and essential safety resources.

JEEVIKA aims to bridge this gap by bringing critical disaster-related information and emergency support resources together in one accessible platform.

From disaster alerts and emergency helplines to safety checklists, shelter information, and family safety tools, JEEVIKA focuses on making emergency preparedness simpler, faster, and more accessible.

🎯 Our Mission

To empower individuals and communities with accessible information, essential safety resources, and reliable emergency preparedness tools that can help reduce confusion and improve decision-making during critical situations.

✨ Key Features

- 🚨 Disaster Alerts: Stay informed about potential disasters and emergency situations.
- 📞 Emergency Helplines: Access important emergency contact information quickly.
- 🛡️ Safety Checklists: Follow essential preparedness guidelines before, during, and after disasters.
- 🏠 Shelter Information: Find information about available emergency shelters and safe locations.
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Safety Tools: Help families stay connected and coordinate during emergencies.
- 🌐 Accessible Information: Bring important humanitarian and disaster preparedness resources together in one platform.

💡 Why JEEVIKA?

During emergencies, access to the right information at the right time can make a significant difference.

JEEVIKA aims to address challenges such as:

- Lack of awareness about disaster preparedness.
- Difficulty finding emergency helplines.
- Limited access to shelter-related information.
- Communication and coordination challenges among family members.
- Difficulty identifying the right safety measures during emergencies.

🌱 Our Vision

We envision a world where every individual has access to essential disaster preparedness resources and the knowledge needed to respond to emergencies with confidence.

JEEVIKA is more than a safety platform — it is a step towards building informed, prepared, and resilient communities.
